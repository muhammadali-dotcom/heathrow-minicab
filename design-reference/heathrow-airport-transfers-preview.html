ARRIVING AT HEATHROW

From arrivals
to your next stop.

Know what happens after landing, where to meet your driver and how waiting time works.

Arrange a Heathrow pickup

Your meeting point

Your pickup may be inside arrivals with a name board or at an agreed pickup location. The arrangement is confirmed with your booking.

Need help? 020 8343 4444 →

After your flight lands

  1. 01

    Clear arrivals and collect your bags

    Allow time for passport control and baggage collection when arranging your pickup.

  2. 02

    Check your confirmed meeting instructions

    Follow the location agreed for your booking. Keep your phone available so you can contact us.

  3. 03

    Meet your driver

    If you cannot find them, call us and tell us your terminal and the nearest clearly signed landmark.

Flight delayed or changed?

We monitor your flight. Please also contact us if your flight is delayed, cancelled or changes so we can confirm your arrangements.

Understand your waiting time

15 minutesincluded waiting

The included waiting period starts when your driver reaches the agreed meeting point. Any waiting rate after this is confirmed before booking.

Pickup questions

What if baggage collection takes longer?

Contact us as soon as you know you need more time. We can confirm the driver’s arrangements and any applicable waiting charges.

What if I cannot find my driver?

Stay in a clearly identifiable location and call 020 8343 4444 or 020 8569 4040. Tell us your terminal and where you are standing.
